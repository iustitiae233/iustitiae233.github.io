<p>MOS 管（MOSFET，Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field Effect Transistor，金属-氧化物-半导体场效应管）走的是另一条路：它是<strong>电压控制型</strong>器件。栅极通过一层极薄的二氧化硅绝缘层与导电沟道隔开，直流状态下几乎不取电流，靠栅源之间的<strong>电场</strong>来控制沟道的通断。一旦沟道打开，撤掉驱动能量它也维持原状（忽略漏电）。</p>

<blockquote>
<p>💡 一句话区分：<strong>三极管吃电流，MOS 管吃电压</strong>。这也是为什么开关电源、电机驱动、电源管理几乎清一色用 MOS 管——栅极不取电流，驱动轻，导通电阻低，损耗小。</p>
</blockquote>
<p>MOS 管有三个主电极：<strong>G（栅极 Gate）</strong>、<strong>D（漏极 Drain）</strong>、<strong>S（源极 Source）</strong>，外加一个<strong>B（衬底 Body / Substrate）</strong>。绝大多数分立 MOS 管在内部把衬底和源极短接，对外只露出 G、D、S 三个脚，所以平时按三端器件处理即可。</p>

<h2>二、基本结构——绝缘栅与导电沟道</h2>
<h3>NMOS（N沟道增强型）</h3>
<p>以最常见的 <strong>N 沟道增强型 MOS 管</strong>为例：</p>
<ul>
<li>取一块 <strong>P 型半导体</strong>做衬底</li>
<li>在衬底上扩散出两个高掺杂的 <strong>N+ 区</strong>，分别是<strong>源极 S</strong> 和<strong>漏极 D</strong></li>
<li>在 S 和 D 之间的衬底表面生长一层二氧化硅（SiO₂）绝缘层，绝缘层上方铺金属，就是<strong>栅极 G</strong></li>
<li>衬底通常与 S 短接</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>沟道是怎么来的：</strong> 栅极 0V 时，S 和 D 之间隔着 P 型衬底，两个 N+ 区被 P 型隔开，像两个背靠背的 PN 结，D-S 之间不导通。当 G 相对 S 加上<strong>正电压</strong> <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>G</mi><mi>S</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_{GS}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8333em;vertical-align:-0.15em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0576em;">GS</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span>，栅极下方的电场会把 P 型衬底里的空穴向下排斥，同时把少数载流子（电子）吸引到紧贴绝缘层的表面。<span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>G</mi><mi>S</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" <blockquote>
<p>🔑 这种"栅极 0V 时无沟道、加正电压才长出沟道"的类型叫<strong>增强型（Enhancement）</strong>，也就是常说的<strong>常闭型</strong>。市面上的分立 MOS 管 99% 都是增强型。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3>PMOS（P沟道增强型）</h3>
<p>把 NMOS 的所有掺杂类型对调：</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>N 型衬底</strong>，两个 <strong>P+ 区</strong>做 S 和 D</li>
<li>G 相对 S 加<strong>负电压</strong> <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>G</mi><mi>S</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_{GS}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8333em;vertical-align:-0.15em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0576em;">GS</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span>，电场把电子排斥、把空穴吸引到表面，形成 <strong>P 型沟道</strong></li>
<li>一切与 NMOS 对偶：NMOS 靠正电压吸引电子，PMOS 靠负电压吸引空穴</li>

<h3>耗尽型（顺带一提）</h3>

<h2>四、DS之间双向导电——MOS管与三极管的一大区别</h2>
<p>三极管的 C-E 之间基本是单向的（反向靠集电结反偏阻断，到击穿才通）。MOS 管不一样：<strong>只要沟道处于导通状态，D 和 S 之间可以双向流过电流</strong>。</p>
<p>原因在于 MOS 管的结构对称性——源极和漏极在物理上都是同型的重掺杂区，沟道对两个方向一视同仁。这和三极管（发射区重掺杂、集电区轻掺杂、基区极薄的不对称结构）根本不同。</p>
<p>但有一个关键限制：<strong>体二极管是单向的</strong>。把沟道和体二极管合起来看，以 NMOS 为例：</p>

<li><strong>电池充放电管理</strong>：充电和放电共用一条 MOS 管通路，电流方向相反但都顺畅</li>
<li><strong>模拟开关 / 传输门</strong>：传递交流小信号，电流方向不停变化</li>
<li><strong>H 桥 / 电机驱动</strong>：每个桥臂的体二极管天然承担续流</li>
</ul>

<h3>4. 低边开关 vs 高边开关</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>低边开关</strong>：MOS 管串在负载和地之间，断开的是回路的地端。负载在断电时仍连着电源正极。</li>
<li><strong>高边开关</strong>：MOS 管串在电源和负载之间，断开的是回路的高端。负载断电后完全脱离电源、保持接地。</li>
</ul>

<ul>
<li><strong>驱动电压要够</strong>：确认 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mi>G</mi></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_G</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8333em;vertical-align:-0.15em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">G</span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span> 能让 MOS 完全导通（标准管 10V，逻辑管 4.5V），别卡在阈值附近</li>

</ul>
<blockquote>
<p>🔌 NMOS 低边开关是驱动继电器、LED、小电机的首选——简单、便宜、好驱动。绝大多数"用单片机控制一个负载"的入门电路都是这个结构。</p>

<h2>九、PMOS防反接电路——靠体二极管启动，靠沟道续命</h2>
<h3>要解决的问题</h3>
<p>电源接反会烧后级。传统的串二极管防反接方案压降 0.3~0.7V，大电流下功耗和发热严重（比如 10A 电流、0.5V 压降就是 5W，二极管烫得不行）。用 PMOS 做防反接，正向时几乎零压降，反向时完全截止。</p>
<h3>接法（关键，与普通高边开关相反！）</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>D 接电源输入端</strong> <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>i</mi><mi>n</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_{in}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8333em;vertical-align:-0.15em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3117em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">in</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></li>
<li><strong>S 接后级负载</strong></li>
<li><strong>G 通过一个电阻接地（GND）</strong></li>
<li>衬底与 S 短接（内部已接好）</li>
</ul>
<blockquote>
<p>⚠️ 注意：这里 <strong>D 接电源、S 接负载</strong>，和第八节高边开关（S 接电源、D 接负载）<strong>正好相反</strong>。原因就是要利用 PMOS 体二极管 <strong>D→S</strong> 的方向来"启动"。这就是 MOS 管 DS 可反向使用的典型例子。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3>工作原理</h3>
<p><strong>正确接线时：</strong></p>
<ol>

<h2>十、补充：实际设计要点与常见坑</h2>
<h3>米勒串扰（dv/dt 误开通）</h3>
<p>桥臂电路里（如 H 桥、半桥），上管高速开通时，高 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mtext>d</mtext><mi>v</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><mtext>d</mtext><mi>t</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\text{d}v/\text{d}t</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1em;vertical-align:-0.25em;"></span><span class="mord text"><span class="mord">d</span></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">v</span><span class="mord">/</span><span class="mord text"><span class="mord">d</span></span><span class="mord mathnormal">t</span></span></span></span> 通过<strong>下管</strong>的 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>C</mi><mrow><mi>G</mi><mi>D</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">C_{GD}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8333em;vertical-align:-0.15em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.0715em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">G</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">D</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span> 耦合到下管栅极，可能瞬间把下管栅极电压拉过阈值，导致<strong>下管误开通</strong>——上下管同时导通，直通短路，炸管。</p>
<p>对策：低阻抗栅极驱动（让栅极电荷快速被泄放/钳位）、合理死区时间、必要时负压关断、选用 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>C</mi><mrow><mi>G</mi><mi>D</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">C_{GD}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8333em;vertical-align:-0.15em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.0715em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">G</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">D</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span> 小的管子。</p>
<h3>寄生振荡</h3>
<p>栅极走线的寄生电感与 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>C</mi><mrow><mi>G</mi><mi>S</mi></mrow></msub><mi mathvariant="normal">/</mi><msub><mi>C</mi><mrow><mi>G</mi><mi>D</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">C_{GS}/C_{GD}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1em;vertical-align:-0.25em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.0715em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0576em;">GS</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span><span class="mord">/</span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.0715em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">G</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">D</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span> 构成 LC 谐振回路，开关瞬间容易激起高频振荡，轻则 EMI 超标，重则栅极过压损坏。</p>
<p>对策：栅极走线<strong>短而粗</strong>、源极回路低感连接、<span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>R</mi><mi>g</mi></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">R_g</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9694em;vertical-align:-0.2861em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0077em;">R</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.1514em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.0077em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">g</span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.2861em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span> 提供阻尼、必要时加小电阻或铁氧体磁珠。</p>
<h3><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>R</mi><mrow><mi>D</mi><mi>S</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>o</mi><mi>n</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">R_{DS(on)}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.0385em;vertical-align:-0.3552em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0077em;">R</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3448em;"><span style="top:-2.5198em;margin-left:-0.0077em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">D</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0576em;">S</span><span class="mopen mtight">(</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">o</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">n</span><span class="mclose mtight">)</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3552em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span> 的正温度系数与并联</h3>
<p><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>R</mi><mrow><mi>D</mi><mi>S</mi><mo class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">D</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0576em;">S</span><span class="mopen mtight">(</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">o</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">n</span><span class="mclose mtight">)</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span <h3>安全工作区（SOA）与雪崩</h3>
<p>MOS 管同时承受高电压和大电流时，受<strong>安全工作区</strong>曲线限制，超出会因过热或二次击穿损坏。感性负载关断时产生的电压尖峰若超过 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>D</mi><mi>S</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_{DS}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8333em;vertical-align:-0.15em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">D</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0576em;">S</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span> 耐压，MOS 管进入<strong>雪崩击穿</strong>状态（自身吸收能量）。偶尔小幅雪崩能扛住，但反复或大幅雪崩会损坏，必须用 TVS 管、RCD 吸收电路或齐纳钳位把尖峰限制住。</p>
<h3>栅极防静电</h3>
<p>栅极氧化层极薄，对静电极其敏感。<strong>存放和焊接 MOS 管时要注意防静电</strong>，未上电的 MOS 管栅极应避免悬空（用导电海绵或短接环保护）。</p>
