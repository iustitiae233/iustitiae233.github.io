3c:Tf612,<h2>一、电容是什么？</h2>
<p>电容的物理结构其实非常简单——<strong>两块导体板中间夹一层绝缘介质</strong>。就像两块大铁板面对面放着，中间隔一层空气或塑料薄膜，这就是一个电容。</p>
<p>电容的核心能力是<strong>储存电荷</strong>。当你给电容两端加上电压，正极板上会积累正电荷，负极板上会积累负电荷，中间隔着绝缘层，电荷跑不掉，于是电能就被"锁"在了电容里面。</p>
<blockquote>
<p>💡 <strong>通俗类比</strong>：电容就像一个<strong>弹性水袋</strong>——你把水压进去，它鼓起来存着；你把外面的压力撤掉，它就慢慢把水放出来。刚灌水时水袋是空的，对水流几乎没有阻力（相当于导线）；灌满了就对水流彻底堵死（相当于断路）。</p>
</blockquote>
<p>电容储电是一个<strong>物理过程</strong>，不是化学反应。所以电容充放电速度极快，寿命也比电池长得多，但能存的能量远远不如电池。</p>
<h3>电容的关键参数</h3>
<p>参数含义<strong>容量 (C)<strong>储电能力，单位法拉 (F)。常用的电容只有微法 (μF)、纳法 (nF)、皮法 (pF)<strong>耐压值</strong>电容能承受的最高电压，超了会击穿甚至爆炸</strong>ESR</strong>等效串联电阻，越小越好，高频电路中很重要<strong>漏电流</strong>电容两端有电压时，微小电流穿过介质的现象</p>
<hr>
<h2>二、核心特性：电容两端电压不能突变</h2>
<p>这是电容最根本、最重要的特性，源自电容的基本公式：</p>
<p><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math <p>为什么呢？如果电压瞬间跳变（<span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>d</mi><mi>t</mi></mrow><annotation <h3>刚上电瞬间，电容可以看作导线</h3>
<p>这是电容"电压不能突变"特性的直接推论。</p>
<p>上电前，电容两端电压为 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>0</mn><mtext>V</mtext></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">0\text{V}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord">0</span><span class="mord text"><span class="mord">V</span></span></span></span></span>。上电瞬间：</p>
<ul>
<li>电源电压突然从 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>0</mn><mtext>V</mtext></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">0\text{V}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord">0</span><span class="mord text"><span class="mord">V</span></span></span></span></span> 跳到 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>5</mn><mtext>V</mtext></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">5\text{V}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord">5</span><span class="mord text"><span class="mord">V</span></span></span></span></span></li>
<li>但电容电压依然是 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>0</mn><mtext>V</mtext></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">0\text{V}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord">0</span><span class="mord text"><span class="mord">V</span></span></span></span></span>（不能突变！）</li>
<li>电容两端出现 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>5</mn><mtext>V</mtext></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">5\text{V}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord">5</span><span class="mord text"><span class="mord">V</span></span></span></span></span> 的电压差</li>
<li>此时电容表现得像<strong>短路/导线</strong>，电流猛冲进去给电容充电</li>
</ul>
<blockquote>
<p>这就是为什么电路板刚通电时会有瞬间大电流（<strong>浪涌电流</strong>），电路设计时必须考虑某个大电容上电时的冲击。</p>
</blockquote>
<p>随着充电进行，电容两端电压缓慢上升，电流逐渐减小。当电压充到接近电源电压时，电流趋于零，此时电容等效为<strong>断路</strong>。</p>
<p>这个"刚上电像导线，充满了像断路"的特性，就是所谓的<strong>隔直通交</strong>：</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>通交</strong>：交流信号（电压不断变化）时，电容反复充放电，总有电流流过——相当于导通</li>
<li><strong>隔直</strong>：直流稳定后，电容电压充到和电源一样，没有电压差就没有电流——相当于断开</li>
</ul>

<h3>实际应用</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>延时灯</strong>：用一个电容 + 电阻给三极管提供基极电压。关灯后，电容慢慢放电，灯不是立刻熄灭而是缓缓变暗。</li>
<li><strong>单片机复位电路</strong>：上电瞬间给 RESET 脚一个短暂的低电平脉冲（电容刚上电像导线，把 RESET 拉低），等电容充满后 RESET 变高电平，芯片开始正常工作。</li>
<li><strong>消抖电路</strong>：按键并联一个小电容，按键抖动产生的短暂毛刺被电容吸收掉，只有持续的高/低电平才能被识别。</li>
</ul>

<h2>四、电容稳压和滤波功能</h2>
<h3>稳压（旁路/去耦）</h3>
<p>这就是每块电路板电源引脚旁边必备那个小电容（通常 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>0.1</mn><mi>μ</mi><mtext>F</mtext></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">0.1\mu\text{F}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8778em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">0.1</span><span class="mord mathnormal">μ</span><span class="mord text"><span class="mord">F</span></span></span></span></span>）的原因。</p>
<p>芯片工作时，它会消耗电流，而且不是稳定消耗——有时多、有时少（比如 CPU 在密集运算时电流飙升）。这种电流的剧烈波动会在电源线上产生电压波动，因为导线本身有电阻和电感。</p>
<p>这时，电源引脚旁的小电容就像一个<strong>本地水库</strong>：</p>
<ul>
<li>芯片突然多要电流 → 电容立刻放电补给，不用走远路去电源那边取</li>
<li>芯片突然少要电流 → 电容吸收多余的电流</li>
</ul>
<p>相当于电容充当了一个<strong>缓冲</strong>，把电源的波动给"压平"了。</p>
<blockquote>
<p>芯片电源引脚 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math encoding="application/x-tex">0.1\mu\text{F} + 10\mu\text{F}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8778em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">0.1</span><span class="mord mathnormal">μ</span><span class="mord text"><span class="mord">F</span></span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8778em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">10</span><span class="mord mathnormal">μ</span><span class="mord text"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>0.1</mn><mi>μ</mi><mtext>F</mtext></mrow><annotation xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>10</mn><mi>μ</mi><mtext>F</mtext></mrow><annotation class="mord">F</span></span></span></span></span> 处理低频波动。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3>滤波</h3>
<p>交流电经整流后得到的是脉动直流——它的大方向是直流，但电压一直在波动，波形像一串连续的半圆。</p>
<p>在整流输出端并联一个足够大的电容：</p>
<ul>
<li>电压上升时，电容充电</li>
<li>电压下降时，电容放电（靠存着的电维持输出电压）</li>
</ul>
<p>结果：原本的脉动波形被"填平"成带微小波纹的平稳直流。这就是<strong>电容滤波</strong>的基本原理。</p>
<p>电容越大，能存越多电 → 放电时电压掉得越少 → 纹波越小。</p>

<p>在交流电路中，电容不仅表现出储存能力，还表现出一种"阻抗"——叫<strong>容抗</strong>，记作 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>X</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mi>c</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">X\_c</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9933em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0785em;">X</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord mathnormal">c</span></span></span></span>。</p>
<p><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>X</mi><mi C}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9933em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord <p>符号含义单位<span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>X</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mi>c</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">X\_c</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9933em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0785em;">X</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord mathnormal">c</span></span></span></span>容抗欧姆 (<span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">Ω</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\Omega</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord">Ω</span></span></span></span>)<span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span></span></span></span>信号频率赫兹 (Hz)<span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>C</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">C</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span></span></span></span>电容容量法拉 (F)</p>
<p><strong>关键洞察</strong>：</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>频率越高，容抗越小</strong> → 高频信号容易通过电容</li>
<li><strong>频率越低，容抗越大</strong> → 低频信号难以通过电容</li>
<li><strong>频率为零（直流），容抗无穷大</strong> → 直流完全通不过</li>
</ul>
<p>这就是"隔直通交"的数学表达。</p>
<blockquote>
<p>举个例子：一个 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>1</mn><mi>μ</mi><mtext>F</mtext></mrow><annotation </blockquote>

<h2>六、RC 低通滤波电路</h2>
<p>低通滤波器——让低频信号通过，把高频信号"滤掉"。</p>
<h3>基本电路</h3>
<blockquote>
<p>输入 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>V</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mrow><mi>i</mi><mi>n</mi></mrow></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V\_{in}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9933em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal">in</span></span></span></span></span> → 电阻 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>R</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">R</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0077em;">R</span></span></span></span> → 输出 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>V</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mrow><mi>o</mi><mi>u</mi><mi>t</mi></mrow></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V\_{out}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9933em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal">o</span><span class="mord mathnormal">u</span><span class="mord mathnormal">t</span></span></span></span></span>，同时 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>V</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mrow><mi>o</mi><mi>u</mi><mi>t</mi></mrow></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V\_{out}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9933em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal">o</span><span class="mord mathnormal">u</span><span class="mord mathnormal">t</span></span></span></span></span> 经电容 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>C</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">C</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span></span></span></span> 到 GND。</p>
</blockquote>
<p>信号先是经过电阻，然后接一个电容到地，输出从电阻和电容的中间接出来。</p>
<h3>原理</h3>
<p><strong>高频信号来的时候</strong>：</p>
<ul>
<li>电容的容抗 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>X</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mi>c</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">X\_c</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9933em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0785em;">X</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord mathnormal">c</span></span></span></span> 非常小（频率高 → 容抗小）</li>
<li>电容相当于把高频信号直接导到地（GND）</li>
<li>输出端几乎没有高频信号 → <strong>高频被滤掉了</strong></li>
</ul>
<p><strong>低频信号来的时候</strong>：</p>
<ul>
<li>电容的容抗 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>X</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mi>c</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">X\_c</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9933em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0785em;">X</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord mathnormal">c</span></span></span></span> 非常大（频率低 → 容抗大）</li>
<li>信号基本走不过电容到地，老老实实走向输出端</li>
<li>输出端低频信号完好 → <strong>低频通过</strong></li>
</ul>
<h3>关键频率：截止频率</h3>
<p><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi><mi C}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.0044em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.1901em;vertical-align:-0.345em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mopen nulldelimiter"></span><span class="mfrac"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.8451em;"><span style="top:-2.655em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:3em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mtight">2</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">π</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0077em;">R</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span></span></span></span><span style="top:-3.23em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:3em;"></span><span class="frac-line" style="border-bottom-width:0.04em;"></span></span><span style="top:-3.394em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:3em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mtight">1</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.345em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span><span class="mclose nulldelimiter"></span></span></span></span></span></p>
<ul>
<li>频率低于 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mi>c</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f\_c</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.0044em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord mathnormal">c</span></span></span></span> 的信号 → 正常通过</li>
<li>频率等于 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mi>c</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f\_c</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.0044em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord mathnormal">c</span></span></span></span> 的信号 → 衰减到原来的 <strong>70.7%</strong></li>
<li>频率高于 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mi>c</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f\_c</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.0044em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord mathnormal">c</span></span></span></span> 的信号 → 逐渐被滤掉，频率越高衰减越厉害</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>肉眼可见的现象（示波器上怎么看？）</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>正弦波输入</strong>：输入一个高频正弦波，你看到输出端<strong>幅度大幅衰减</strong>（变小）；输入一个低频正弦波，输出端幅度几乎<strong>无损通过</strong>。</li>
<li><strong>方波输入（最关键现象）</strong>：给一个方波（棱角分明），输出端会变成<strong>圆润的“梯形波”或“三角波”</strong>。因为方波的“棱角”包含极其丰富的高频分量，都被电容“抹平”了。</li>
<li><strong>相移现象</strong>：输出信号会比输入信号<strong>滞后</strong>（延迟）一个角度。频率越高，滞后越严重，最高接近-90°。</li>
</ul>
<h3>应用场景</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>音频电路</strong>：滤掉高频噪声和嘶嘶声</li>
<li><strong>ADC 输入前端</strong>：滤掉高于采样频率一半的噪声（抗混叠滤波）</li>
<li><strong>传感器信号调理</strong>：过滤环境电磁干扰</li>
<li><strong>电源去噪</strong>：滤除电源线上的高频纹波</li>
</ul>

<h2>七、RC 高通滤波电路</h2>
<p>与低通相反——高频通过，低频滤掉。</p>
<h3>基本电路</h3>
<blockquote>
<p>输入 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>V</mi><mi aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9933em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal">in</span></span></span></span></span> → 电容 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>C</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">C</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span></span></span></span> → 输出 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>V</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mrow><mi>o</mi><mi>u</mi><mi>t</mi></mrow></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V\_{out}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9933em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal">o</span><span class="mord mathnormal">u</span><span class="mord mathnormal">t</span></span></span></span></span>，同时 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>V</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mrow><mi>o</mi><mi>u</mi><mi>t</mi></mrow></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V\_{out}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9933em;vertical-align:-0.31em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="mord" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal">o</span><span class="mord mathnormal">u</span><span class="mord mathnormal">t</span></span></span></span></span> 经电阻 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>R</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">R</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0077em;">R</span></span></span></span> 到 GND。</p>
</blockquote>
<p>信号先经过电容，输出端接一个电阻到地。</p>
<h3>原理</h3>
<p><strong>高频信号来的时候</strong>：</p>
<ul>
<li>电容容抗非常小，高频信号轻松穿过电容</li>
<li>到达输出端，高频信号完好 → <strong>高频通过</strong></li>
</ul>
<p><strong>低频信号来的时候</strong>：</p>
<ul>
<li>电容容抗非常大，信号很难穿过电容</li>
<li>输出端几乎收不到信号 → <strong>低频被滤掉</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>直流信号：电容相当于断路，完全通不过。</p>

<p><strong>肉眼可见的现象（示波器上怎么看？）</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>正弦波输入</strong>：输入一个高频正弦波，输出端<strong>幅度基本不变</strong>；输入一个低频正弦波，输出端<strong>幅度急剧缩小</strong>。频率越低，幅度越小，直流量直接归零。</li>
<li><strong>方波输入（最关键现象）</strong>：给一个恒定的方波（比如0~3.3V），输出端会变成<strong>正负对称的“尖峰”或“毛刺”</strong>！方波的“平顶”（直流分量）被电容吃掉了，只剩下<strong>上升沿和下降沿的瞬间跳变</strong>（尖端放电）。这就是“求微分”的物理表现。</li>
<li><strong>相移现象</strong>：输出信号比输入信号<strong>超前</strong>（提前）一个角度。频率越低，超前越明显，最高接近+90°。</li>
</ul>
<h3>应用场景</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>音频放大器输入耦合</strong>：把前后级的直流工作点隔离开（只让音频交流信号通过，不让直流偏置电压传递）</li>
<li><strong>示波器 AC 耦合模式</strong>：隔离被测信号的直流分量，只看交流部分</li>
<li><strong>去除传感器信号的直流漂移</strong>：只保留变化的成分</li>
</ul>
