<h3>与运算放大器的区别</h3>
<p>比较器和运放在符号上一模一样（都是 +、- 两个输入端），但它们是<strong>为不同任务设计的两种芯片</strong>，不能简单互换：</p>

<li>把缓慢变化的传感器信号转成数字触发</li>

<h2>二、输出结构——为什么比较器常常需要上拉电阻 ★</h2>
<p>这一节回答最常被问到的问题：<strong>比较器输出到底要不要加上拉电阻？</strong> 答案取决于它内部的输出级结构。比较器输出级有两种典型形式。</p>
<h3>1. 推挽输出（Push-Pull / 图腾柱）</h3>
<p>内部有两个输出管一上一下：</p>
<ul>
<li>上管导通时，把输出<strong>拉高</strong>到正电源</li>
<li>下管导通时，把输出<strong>拉低</strong>到地</li>
</ul>
<p>两管轮流工作，<strong>能主动输出高电平，也能主动输出低电平</strong>，不需要任何外部元件。输出电压由比较器自己的供电决定。</p>
<blockquote>
<p>🔑 推挽输出的比较器<strong>不需要上拉电阻</strong>，接上负载直接用。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3>2. 开漏 / 开集输出（OD / OC）</h3>
<p>内部<strong>只有一个下拉管</strong>，没有上拉管：</p>
<ul>
<li>输出低电平：下拉管导通，把输出<strong>拉到地</strong>（低电平有驱动能力）</li>
<li>输出高电平：下拉管截止，输出端<strong>悬空</strong>（高阻态）——<strong>完全靠外部上拉电阻把电压拉上去</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>换句话说，开漏/开集结构<strong>自己给不出高电平</strong>。</p>
<blockquote>
<p>⚠️ OD/OC 输出的比较器<strong>必须外接上拉电阻</strong>，否则输出永远只能在地电平和高阻之间跳，永远到不了真正的高电平。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3>什么时候必须加上拉电阻 ★（核心问题）</h3>
<p>把上面的结论归纳成几条判别规则：</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>输出是 OD/OC 结构 → 必须加上拉电阻</strong>，否则没有高电平</li>
<li><strong>输出是推挽结构 → 不需要上拉电阻</strong>，加了反而可能和内部上管打架</li>
<li><strong>看数据手册</strong>：输出类型写 "Open Drain" / "Open Collector" 就是开路输出，要加上拉；写 "Push-Pull" / "Totem Pole" 就是推挽，不用加</li>
<li><strong>经验型号</strong>：<strong>LM393、LM339 系列</strong>是经典的<strong>开集</strong>输出，几乎一定要加上拉电阻。这是新手最容易踩的坑——电路接好了，输出永远只有低电平，查半天才发现没加上拉</li>
</ol>
<blockquote>
<p>💡 一个简单的自检：如果比较器输出低电平正常、高电平却上不去（万用表量出来在 0V 附近或乱飘），<strong>第一个怀疑的就是漏了上拉电阻</strong>。</p>
</blockquote>

<h2>四、输入信号范围——共模电压的禁区</h2>
<h3>共模输入电压范围 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>C</mi><mi>M</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_{CM}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8333em;vertical-align:-0.15em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.109em;">M</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></h3>
<p>比较器<strong>两个输入端的电压都必须落在允许的共模输入范围内</strong>，否则性能恶化甚至输出错误。数据手册里这个参数写作 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>C</mi><mi>M</mi><mi>R</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_{CMR}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8333em;vertical-align:-0.15em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.109em;">M</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0077em;">R</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span> 或 "Common-Mode Input Voltage Range"。</p>
<p>注意是"两个输入端"——不仅被测信号要合规，<strong>参考电压也要在范围内</strong>。</p>
<h3>超出范围的后果</h3>
<ul>
<li>输入级差分对退出正常偏置区 → 增益急剧下降，翻转阈值偏移</li>
<li>严重时发生<strong>相位反转（Phase Inversion）</strong>：输出跳到与真实比较结果<strong>相反</strong>的极性。比如本该输出高，却输出低，逻辑完全颠倒</li>
<li>这类错误非常隐蔽，仿真里未必体现，上电才暴露</li>
</ul>

<h3>Rail-to-Rail（轨到轨）输入</h3>
<p>要覆盖 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>0</mn><mo>∼</mo><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>C</mi><mi>C</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">0 \sim V_{CC}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">0</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">∼</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8333em;vertical-align:-0.15em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span> 全程，需要<strong>轨到轨输入</strong>。这类比较器内部并联了两套差分对（一套管低端、一套管高端），在全范围都能工作。当信号可能接近电源轨时，应优先选 Rail-to-Rail 型号。</p>
<h3>设计建议</h3>
<ul>
<li>估算被测信号和参考电压在比较器输入端实际呈现的电压范围，<strong>确认都落在 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>C</mi><mi>M</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_{CM}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8333em;vertical-align:-0.15em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3283em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0715em;">C</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.109em;">M</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.15em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span> 内</strong></li>
<li>超范围时：用电阻分压把信号缩进范围、换用 Rail-to-Rail 比较器、或调整参考电平位置</li>
<li>留意"相位反转"这个隐藏陷阱，输入绝不能长时间压在范围之外</li>
</ul>

<h2>五、颤振（Chattering）——缓慢信号过阈值时的麻烦 ★</h2>
<h3>现象</h3>
<p>当输入信号<strong>缓慢变化</strong>经过比较器的翻转阈值时，叠加在信号上的噪声会让输入电压在阈值上下快速来回。比较器增益极高，毫伏级噪声就被放大成满幅翻转，于是输出不再是干净的跳变，而是在 0 和 1 之间<strong>高频抖动</strong>——这就是颤振（也叫振荡、抖动）。</p>
<p>后级的计数器会乱跳、中断会被反复触发、继电器会啪啪打火——一切依赖"输出跳一次"的逻辑都会出错。</p>
<h3>成因</h3>
<ul>
<li>单阈值比较器<strong>只有一个翻转点</strong></li>
<li>信号缓慢爬升到阈值附近时，哪怕 1mV 的噪声都能让输入在阈值上下穿越多次</li>
<li>比较器响应又快，于是输出疯狂翻转</li>
</ul>
<pre><code class="hljs language-cpp">输入信号 ───────╱╲ 噪声 ╱╱─────── 缓慢上升过阈值
阈值 ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─

输出   ─┐┌┐┌┐┌──────────────── 颤振！反复翻转
</code></pre>
<h3>解决方案</h3>
<h4>方案 1：迟滞（正反馈）——首选 ★</h4>
<p>引入<strong>施密特触发器</strong>结构：从输出端接一个<strong>正反馈电阻</strong>到同相输入端，让翻转阈值随当前输出状态而变。结果是产生<strong>两个阈值</strong>：</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>上升阈值 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>t</mi><mi>h</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mi>h</mi><mi>i</mi><mi>g</mi><mi>h</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_{th\_high}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.0503em;vertical-align:-0.367em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3361em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">t</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">h</span><span class="mord mtight" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">hi</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">g</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">h</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.367em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></strong>：信号上升时，必须超过它，输出才翻高</li>
<li><strong>下降阈值 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>t</mi><mi>h</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">_</mi><mi>l</mi><mi>o</mi><mi>w</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_{th\_low}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.0503em;vertical-align:-0.367em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3361em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">t</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">h</span><span class="mord mtight" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">_</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0197em;">l</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">o</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0269em;">w</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.367em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></strong>：信号下降时，必须低于它，输出才翻低</li>
<li>两者之差 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9694em;vertical-align:-0.2861em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3361em;"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">h</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">y</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">s</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.2861em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span><span class="mspace" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">t</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">h</span><span </ul>
<p>只要噪声幅度小于 <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>h</mi><mi>y</mi><mi>s</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_{hys}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9694em;vertical-align:-0.2861em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3361em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">h</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">y</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">s</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.2861em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span>，输出就稳如磐石，不会颤振。</p>
<blockquote>
<p>🔑 迟滞的核心思想：<strong>"请神容易送神难"</strong>。一旦翻到高，就要降很多才肯回低；一旦翻到低，就要升很多才肯回高。两个阈值之间的"死区"把噪声吞掉了。</p>
</blockquote>
<p>设计原则：<span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>V</mi><mrow><mi>h</mi><mi>y</mi><mi>s</mi></mrow></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">V_{hys}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.9694em;vertical-align:-0.2861em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.2222em;">V</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.3361em;"><span style="top:-2.55em;margin-left:-0.2222em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight"><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">h</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">y</span><span class="mord mathnormal mtight">s</span></span></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.2861em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span> 要<strong>大于信号上的噪声峰峰值</strong>，但又不能大到影响判决精度（迟滞太大，判决点会明显偏移）。</p>
<h4>方案 2：RC 低通滤波</h4>
<p>在输入端或输出端加 RC 滤波，滤除高频噪声。</p>
<ul>
<li>优点：简单</li>
<li>缺点：引入延迟、拖慢响应，治标不治本，适合噪声主要是高频分量的场合</li>
</ul>
<h4>方案 3：后级消抖 / 锁存</h4>
<p>用软件消抖（采样多次判定）或 D 触发器锁存，适合慢变信号（如按键、温度阈值）。</p>
<blockquote>
<p>实践中，<strong>方案 1（迟滞）是最干净的硬件解法</strong>，几乎所有"正经"的比较器电路都会按需加一点迟滞。</p>
</blockquote>
